about every 60 s; three in a row end the thread, and timeouts also change the rival's offer ID.
In practice case P05 this caused five timeouts, including on the supplier that had already agreed.
- First report whether the merged runner already sends to several threads concurrently.
- Implement, behind the flag: when another live thread holds an acceptable or agreed offer and its
  clock is older than ~35 s, send that thread a short holding message that repeats our last term
  sheet exactly (or carries no term sheet). Rounds are not scarce under an 8-minute cap, and a
  holding message beats a timeout. Do this concurrently with the main move if the runner supports it.
- Test with the latency probe on S02 and a unit test. The result must show fewer timeouts and no
  regression in score or closing.
